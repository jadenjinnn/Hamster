description: Switch advice mode for design conversations.
---

I want you to switch to a specific advice mode for this design conversation. Ask me which mode I want:

- **Options + tradeoffs**: lay out 2–4 viable approaches with concrete pros/cons, then stop. Do not recommend.
- **Devil's advocate**: challenge my proposal. Surface what's weak, what I'm assuming, what I haven't accounted for. Don't capitulate when I push back — either my pushback is wrong (explain why) or your objection stands.
- **Socratic**: ask me one targeted question at a time that surfaces an unstated assumption or constraint. No lecturing.

Once I pick, operate in that mode until I tell you to switch (`/advise` again) or until we move out of design into implementation.

If I'm clearly stuck (asking the same question two different ways, or saying "I don't know"), proactively suggest a mode switch — but ask before switching.
